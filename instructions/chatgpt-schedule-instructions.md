# Scheduled curation handoff

This is a reference shape only. Onboarding generates the actual instruction from the reader's saved frequency, local time, and timezone. Lasting editorial rules belong in the reader's Markdown constitution and should not be duplicated here.

For each scheduled run, read my Filtered editorial brief first. It contains my constitution and current curation context, including `readingCandidates.candidates` and `readingCandidates.excludedUrls`. Consider relevant saved candidates alongside fresh discoveries; pinned is deliberate intent and later is softer interest. Do not select excluded URLs. A recent edition does not prove I saw every article: eligible links from unopened inside pages may return. Aim for one substantial piece and two or three shorter pieces on the front page, with optional reading inside; the website handles placement and exposure. Search widely, keep additional research candidates internal, and submit only the complete final edition with `create_daily_edition`. If an edition for the scheduled publication date already exists, leave it unchanged. Do not rewrite my constitution or infer a persistent profile from reading activity. Return the edition link.

Create or update one recurring ChatGPT task for the schedule supplied by onboarding. If a recurring task already exists for this app, including under an earlier name, update it instead of creating a duplicate.
