# Current-priority editorial weighting

As a default:
- Give current priorities roughly **60–70%** of interest-driven reading time.
- Do not allow one active project to dominate the edition for many consecutive days.
- Prefer material that connects two or more current priorities.
- Continue to reserve explicit space for world context, constructive news, and serendipity.
- If a current priority stops generating action, learning, or genuine curiosity, reduce its weight rather than feeding it indefinitely.
